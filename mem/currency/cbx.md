---
name: CBX currency system
description: CBX is the site's internal currency; 4 CBX equals 1 USD, with golden coin-only displays and up to two decimals.
type: feature
---
CashBullX uses CBX as its internal currency everywhere.
- Fixed conversion: 4 CBX = 1 USD.
- Existing balances and all historical internal monetary records must be converted at 4× without changing economic value.
- User-facing CBX amounts use a golden coin icon with no dollar symbol and no repeated textual prefix.
- Show up to two decimal places and hide unnecessary trailing zeroes.
- Deposits remain actual USDT blockchain payments but credit the equivalent CBX amount.
- Withdrawals are entered/displayed in CBX and paid as the equivalent USDT amount.
